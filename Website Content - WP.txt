I. 

The problem
For most people, making a will or setting up an estate plan feels complicated, expensive, and emotionally overwhelming. So they postpone it until it's too late.
The existing solutions are fragmented, overly transactional, and rarely built for evolving, cross-border families. They lead to generations of loved ones left to deal with preventable conflict, confusion, and legal chaos. 
The solution: Will Patra isn’t just a tool; it’s a complete, end-to-end experience. 
Whether you want to start today, explore your options, or walk the full journey with support, we meet you where you are and grow with you as your life evolves.
From the first clause of your will to the last transfer of property, we make sure it all flows smoothly, legally, and with dignity. As they say, “A stitch in time saves nine”. 
Who are we:
Will Patra is your trusted companion in the world of succession planning (a space too often clouded by confusion, anxiety, and delay). We're reimagining it with clarity, ease, and care. Our mission is simple: Make planning for the future feel as natural and empowering as planning for your day tomorrow.
With a smart blend of intuitive technology and human support, Will Patra helps families take control of what matters most to them: the protection of generational wealth and the security of their family.

Where it all began 
It all started with a father in India with two children overseas, and one big question: “How do I transfer everything smoothly, when my children aren’t even in the same country?”
What followed was a maze of legal advisors, tax consultants, and cross-border confusion. The process was expensive, disjointed, and full of uncertainty.
That’s when we realised: Estate planning doesn’t have to be this hard.
So we built WillPatra; first, with the thought of being a platform for cross-border Indian families, but as we spoke to more people, we saw a bigger gap: most Indians faced this issue because it felt overwhelming, unaffordable, or too uncomfortable to think about until it was urgent.
Finally, we created WillPatra into a platform that blends expert support and intuitive technology to help families take control of what matters most to them: the protection of generational wealth and the security of their family without fear, confusion, or chaos. 
And this is just the beginning.
